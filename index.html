The Photo Archive

From the River
to the Railroad

Preserving our past and inspiring a new generation of storytellers.

Founded on June 3, 1980, the Historical Society of Millersburg & Upper Paxton Township is a 501(c)(3) non-profit that fosters interest in the history of Millersburg and the surrounding area. The Susquehanna River, the railroad, and the region's Native American heritage all shape who we are.

This photo archive puts the Society's collection online: every photograph is indexed by year, place, subject, and donor so members and visitors can search it from anywhere.

  • Conduct Research

    Help members and visitors trace a long-ago relative or explore the area's past.

  • Collect & Preserve

    Care for tens of thousands of records and objects of historic significance.

  • Site Preservation

    Mark, preserve, and restore local sites, including the Museum building and the train depot.

  • Engage the Community

    Share the history, culture, and art of our community with everyone.

Millersburg

History

  1. ~19,000–8,000 B.C.

    Paleo-Indian peoples, the region's first inhabitants, settle the Susquehanna River basin at the close of the last Ice Age.

  2. 1790

    Daniel Miller, drawn to the spot where Wiconisco Creek meets the Susquehanna, receives a deed for about 979 acres at the creek's mouth.

  3. c. 1800

    Ferry service to the west shore begins. Miller reserves the ferry rights in 1807 and sells them to David Kramer in 1826.

  4. 1807

    The town plan, surveyed by Peter Williamson, is filed with Dauphin County: 269 lots, three church lots, Market Square, and parkland.

  5. 1834

    The Lykens Valley Railroad sends its first coal shipment to the river, where barges are poled across to the Pennsylvania Canal.

  6. 1846

    The 12-mile Wiconisco Canal opens, later adding basins, wharves, loading trestles, and a lime kiln at Millersburg.

  7. 1857

    The Northern Central Railroad reaches Millersburg from Harrisburg, joining several rail lines at the Summit Branch Hotel and depot.

  8. 1898

    The new passenger station on Center Street opens; the Niagara Express is the first train to stop there on April 1.

Visit the Museum

The Historical Society Museum is at 330 Center Street in Millersburg, across the street from the Millersburg Firehouse. Volunteer staff offer tours of the museum on request, and the library and genealogy resources are open to visitors.
